My taste in music

My taste in music is at the same time diverse and specific, as it can span all kinds of genres but the music's content and context needs to vibe with me personally. I won't like music simply because it belongs to a certain genre. However, certain genres have gotten less of my ear-time (rap/hip-hop, EDM, world music) than others (rock, metal, indie).

I like it when songs have a dynamic composition (meaning changes in tempo, mood, expression throughout the song). I prefer non-screamed vocals, although there are screamed vocals that I like or don't mind (like in Cult of Luna or Hippotraktor). However, vocals (clean or scream) are always a bit hit or miss with me.
