# Games


- **>Observer\_** (grimdark, sci-fi, narrative-focused creepy game)
- **Bramble: The Mountain King** (dark, cinematic fairytale platformer with boss battles)
- **Control** (creepy SCP-like, third-person adventure)
- **Hypnospace Outlaw** (comprehensive 90’s webpage/geocities/computer-interface simulator with a narrative and a lot of music)
- **Luck Be A Landlord** (roguelike deckbuilding slot machine; earn money through gambling to pay ever increasing rent)
- **Outer Wilds** (superb open interplanetary explorer with a time-loop mechanic)
- **Oxygen Not Included** (2D survival colony builder / resource manager with deep physics simulations)
- **Return of the Obra Dinn** (deduction and mystery-solving game on a 19th century ship)
- **Sea Of Solitude** (semi-open world, narrative-focused explorer) 
- **Simmiland** (card-based god-game; change environments to influence people into adapting and evolving)
- **Slay The Spire** (roguelike deckbuilder card-game) 
- **Sniper Elite 4** (third-person shooter with huge open maps) 
- **Sonic & All-Stars Racing Transformed** (Sonic’s kart-racing battler with diverse tracks based on multiple different games and characters)
- **Supraland** + **Supraland: Six Feet Under** (First-person puzzle platformer with intricate mechanics in an open world)
- **The Case of the Golden Idol** (2D point-and-click deductive, mad-lib style puzzler with a 18th century cult theme)
- **The Witcher 3** + DLC’s (Open world, slavic fantasy RPG) 
- **The Witness** (open world, exploration and puzzle game)

Zachtronics games - puzzle games with a lot of possible solutions where optimization is encouraged:

- **Shenzhen I/O** (assemble hardware pieces and write code for each to calculate the requested output)
- **Infinifactory** (build assembly lines in a 3D first-person environment, to make the requested output from the provided input)
- **SpaceChem** (set up the machinery and instructions for building the requested molecules.
- **Opus Magnum** (link alchemical nodes together to form the requested substance)
