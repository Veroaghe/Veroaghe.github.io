Here's a list of some of my favorite comic, manga and strip series. Some titles are in Nederlands (Dutch), my native language.
I've added some names of people who worked on each serie, but it's not a complete representation.

- **De Duistere Steden** – François Schuiten, Benoît Peeters
- **De Holle Aardes** – François Schuiten, Luc Schuiten
- **The Goon** – Eric Powell
- **Metabaronnen** – Juan Gimenez, Alejandro Jodorowsky
- **Soda** – Bruno Gazotti, Tome
- **Rachel Rising** – Terry Moore
- **Hellblazer** – Jamie Delano
- **De Klaagzang Van De Verloren Gewesten** – Phillipe Delaby
- **Elektra: Assassin** – Frank Miller, Bill Sienkiewicz
- **De Wereld Van Edena** – Moebius
- **Buddha** – Osamu Tezuka
- **Ode aan Kirihito** – Osamu Tezuka
- **De Chninkel** – Grzegorz Rosiński, Jean Van Hamme
- **Brooklyn Dreams** – J.M.DeMatteis, Glenn Barr
- **Attack On Titan** – Hajime Isayama
- **Coda** – Simon Spurrier, Matìas Bergara
